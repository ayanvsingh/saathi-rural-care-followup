Saathi: ASHA Follow-Up Tracker

Saathi is a prototype app that helps ASHA health workers in rural India keep track of patients with chronic conditions like diabetes and hypertension, and makes sure they come back for follow-up care.

Prototype:  https://ayanvsingh.github.io/saathi-rural-care-followup/

The problem it addresses

In rural and semi-urban India, many people who are screened or diagnosed with diabetes or hypertension never return for a follow-up visit. Published studies show that only about 6% of high-risk people flagged in one large screening program completed confirmatory testing, and only about 35% of screened patients in a hypertension program returned for even one follow-up. The most common reason isn’t cost or distance. Patients feel fine, so they don’t see the need to go back. Without follow-up, a manageable condition can lead to preventable complications.

India has roughly 7.19 lakh ASHAs (community health workers) who are responsible for following up with these patients. But they are overloaded and have no simple tool to see who is due, who has missed a visit, and who needs urgent attention.

What Saathi does

Gives each ASHA a daily dashboard showing who is due today, overdue, or escalated
Keeps a searchable patient list with village, condition, latest vitals and days overdue
Generates SMS and WhatsApp reminders for patients who are due, with their consent
Sends the ASHA her own morning digest, so she is nudged too
Escalates patients who are more than 7 days overdue to the PHC supervisor
Gives supervisors an overview of every ASHA’s caseload and on-time rate
Lets an ASHA add a new patient in under a minute, recording whose phone is on file and their preferred channel

Approach

Saathi is designed to sit on top of the ASHA workforce and government NCD tools that already exist, rather than replace them. It keeps costs low, works over plain SMS for patients without smartphones, and builds messaging consent into onboarding.
